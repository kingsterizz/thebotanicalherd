🌱 Botany Notes

Plant Structure

  • Plants consist of roots, stems, and leaves
  • Roots absorb water and nutrients from soil
  • Stems transport resources and provide support
  • Leaves are the primary site of photosynthesis

Photosynthesis

  • Process: Light energy + H₂O + CO₂ → Glucose + O₂
  • Occurs in chloroplasts within leaf cells
  • Light-dependent reactions occur in thylakoids
  • Light-independent reactions (Calvin Cycle) occur in stroma

Plant Classification

  • Non-vascular plants: Mosses and liverworts
  • Seedless vascular plants: Ferns
  • Seed plants: Gymnosperms and Angiosperms
  • Angiosperms are the most diverse group (flowering plants)

Plant Reproduction

  • Asexual: Vegetative reproduction (runners, bulbs, fragmentation)
  • Sexual: Production of seeds through pollination and fertilization
  • Double fertilization in angiosperms
  • Seeds contain embryo and stored nutrients

🦁 Zoology Notes

Animal Classification

  • Invertebrates: Over 97% of animal species (insects, worms, mollusks)
  • Vertebrates: Fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, mammals
  • Vertebrates share backbone/spine and internal skeleton
  • Each group has unique adaptations for their environment

Animal Anatomy

  • Body systems: Skeletal, muscular, nervous, circulatory, digestive
  • Endocrine and reproductive systems
  • Homeostasis maintained through organ system coordination
  • Adaptation structures vary by species and environment

Animal Behavior

  • Instinctive behavior: Fixed responses (migration, nesting)
  • Learned behavior: Acquired through experience
  • Social behavior: Cooperation, competition, communication
  • Territorial and reproductive displays

Adaptations

  • Structural: Physical features for survival (camouflage, wings, claws)
  • Behavioral: Actions that aid survival (hibernation, hunting)
  • Physiological: Internal processes (temperature regulation, metabolism)
  • Result of natural selection over many generations

🌍 Ecology Notes

Ecosystems

  • Community of organisms + their physical environment
  • Biotic factors: Living organisms
  • Abiotic factors: Non-living environment (temperature, light, water)
  • Energy flows and nutrients cycle through ecosystems

Food Chains & Webs

  • Producer → Primary Consumer → Secondary Consumer → Tertiary Consumer
  • Decomposers break down dead matter
  • Energy decreases at each trophic level (~10% rule)
  • Complex food webs show multiple feeding relationships

Biodiversity

  • Genetic diversity: Variation within species
  • Species diversity: Number of different species
  • Ecosystem diversity: Variety of habitats and ecosystems
  • High biodiversity increases ecosystem stability and resilience

Conservation

  • Habitat preservation and restoration
  • Species protection and recovery programs
  • Sustainable resource management
  • Reducing pollution and greenhouse gas emissions

🔬 Cell Biology Notes

Cell Structure

  • Prokaryotes: Bacteria, no nucleus (single-celled)
  • Eukaryotes: Nucleus + organelles (animals and plants)
  • Cell membrane: Selectively permeable boundary
  • Nucleus: Contains DNA and controls cell activities

Organelles

  • Mitochondria: ATP production (energy)
  • Chloroplasts: Photosynthesis in plant cells
  • Endoplasmic reticulum: Protein/lipid synthesis
  • Golgi apparatus: Packaging and transport
  • Lysosomes: Cellular digestion and waste

Cell Division

  • Mitosis: Produces two identical daughter cells (growth, repair)
  • Meiosis: Produces four haploid gametes (sexual reproduction)
  • Interphase: DNA replication and growth
  • Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase: Mitotic stages

Cellular Transport

  • Diffusion: Movement from high to low concentration
  • Osmosis: Water movement across membrane
  • Active transport: Requires energy (ATP)
  • Endocytosis and exocytosis: Bulk transport

🧬 Genetics Notes

DNA Structure

  • Double helix structure with complementary base pairs
  • Adenine pairs with Thymine (A-T)
  • Guanine pairs with Cytosine (G-C)
  • Sugar-phosphate backbone provides structural support

Gene Expression

  • Transcription: DNA → mRNA in nucleus
  • Translation: mRNA → Protein at ribosomes
  • Genetic code: Triplet codons specify amino acids
  • One gene = one protein (usually)

Inheritance Patterns

  • Dominant traits: Expressed in heterozygotes
  • Recessive traits: Only in homozygotes
  • Mendelian inheritance: Laws of segregation and assortment
  • Punnett squares predict offspring genotypes

Evolution & Variation

  • Mutations: Permanent changes in DNA sequence
  • Natural selection: Organisms with favorable traits reproduce
  • Genetic drift: Random changes in allele frequency
  • Speciation: Populations diverge into separate species

🧠 Human Biology Notes

Nervous System

  • Central: Brain and spinal cord
  • Peripheral: Somatic (voluntary) and autonomic (involuntary)
  • Neurons: Cell body, dendrites, axon
  • Synapses: Gaps where neurotransmitters transmit signals

Circulatory System

  • Heart: 4 chambers pump blood
  • Arteries: Carry oxygenated blood from heart
  • Veins: Return deoxygenated blood to heart
  • Capillaries: Exchange site for nutrients and gases

Respiratory System

  • Nasal passages → Trachea → Bronchi → Lungs
  • Gas exchange: O₂ in, CO₂ out
  • Diaphragm: Main breathing muscle
  • Hemoglobin in RBCs carries oxygen

Digestive System

  • Mouth → Esophagus → Stomach → Small intestine → Large intestine
  • Enzymes break down food into absorbable nutrients
  • Small intestine: Primary site of nutrient absorption
  • Pancreas and liver aid digestion

Endocrine System

  • Glands produce and release hormones
  • Pituitary: Master gland controlling other glands
  • Thyroid: Metabolism regulation
  • Pancreas: Blood sugar regulation (insulin/glucagon)
