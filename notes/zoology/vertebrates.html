What are Vertebrates?

Vertebrates are animals with a backbone or spinal column. They represent about 3% of all animal species but include the largest animals on Earth like whales, elephants, and giraffes.

Key Features

  • Backbone made of vertebrae
  • Internal skeleton (endoskeleton)
  • Bilateral body symmetry
  • Complex nervous system with brain
  • Closed circulatory system
